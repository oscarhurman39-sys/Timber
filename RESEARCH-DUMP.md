# Research dump — the 51 wishlist plants

Paste-ready batches for your research tool. Three batches of 17 covers the lot.

Each batch matches the add pipeline: research 17, validate 17, add 17 with
`--quick` (~17s), then one `node tests/run-all.js --jobs 3` before pushing.

**Send `PLANT-BRIEF.md` with each batch.** It now carries the two rules that cost
cleanup on every plant supplied so far: the soil pair has measured length limits
(26 and 44 characters), and no field value may end in a citation number.
