## Full description (4000)
Plant Cards turns learning garden plants into a card game.

Every card is one plant: a real photograph, its common and Latin names,
hardiness rating, height and spread, flowering months, and at-a-glance
ratings for thirst, care level, pest risk and growth speed. Flip the card
for the trade sheet: soil, aspect, watering, pruning and what to tell a
customer.

Swipe right when you know it, left to see it again. Cards you skip come
back; cards you learn drop out of the deck, so the pile gets smaller as you
get better. A spaced-repetition review brings each plant back just before
you would forget it.

• 430+ plants, from bedding to trees, with more added regularly
• Real photographs of the actual plants, not stock images
• Quiz mode: name the plant from its Latin name and build a streak
• Search any plant and read its full care sheet
• "Listen" plays the Latin pronunciation using your phone's own voice
• Works fully offline once loaded
• No account, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking — your progress stays on
  your phone

Built for garden-centre staff, horticulture students and anyone who wants
to walk through a nursery and know what they are looking at.

The app is also published on the web as "Timber". Same deck, same
progress rules.
