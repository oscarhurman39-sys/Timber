Plant Cards — Privacy policy

Also published as "Timber". Last updated 28 September 2026.

Plant Cards is a study app for learning garden plants by swiping through cards. It has no user accounts and no server of its own. Nothing you do in the app leaves your device.

What the app stores

Your progress is saved in your device's local browser storage and nowhere else:

  • which cards you have marked as learned or skipped, and your recently viewed cards;
  • your quiz results and best streak;
  • your spaced-repetition review dates;
  • a small diagnostic log of app errors, which stays on your device unless you choose to copy or share it yourself.

Uninstall the app, or clear its site data, and all of this is deleted. There is no copy anywhere else and no way for us to recover it.

What the app sends

The only network requests the app makes are to plantcards-app.github.io, to download its own files (the deck, the artwork and the photographs) and to keep a copy of them on your device so it works offline. It does not contact any third party, does not load advertising, and does not run analytics or tracking of any kind.

The app is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record standard server logs (such as IP addresses) when files are downloaded; see GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive or see those logs.

Permissions

The app never asks for your camera, location, contacts, microphone or notifications. The "Listen" button uses the text-to-speech engine already on your phone; the words are spoken locally and sent nowhere. The "Share" button uses your phone's own share sheet, and only shares the plant text you chose to share.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, of any age. It contains no user-generated content, no chat and no purchases.
